# NairaIntel — Product Specification

**Last updated:** 2026-08-06  
**Status:** Active direction

---

## 1. Strategic Positioning

### What NairaIntel is

NairaIntel is a **premium Nigerian investment intelligence platform**.

It combines:
- Portfolio tracking
- AI-powered market analysis
- A focused investor community
- Structured financial education

into one high-signal product for Nigerian investors.

### Positioning statement

> **NairaIntel is where serious Nigerian investors track their portfolio, get intelligent market insights, discuss stocks & strategies with peers, and level up their knowledge — all in one premium experience.**

### What NairaIntel is *not*

NairaIntel is **not** trying to become another Nairaland.

| Dimension | Nairaland | NairaIntel |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Scope | Broad Nigerian public square | Narrow: investing & capital markets |
| Dominant content | Politics, Romance, Jobs, Entertainment | Stocks, strategies, fixed income, personal finance |
| Tone | Chaotic, high-volume, highly political | Professional, insightful, premium |
| Core value | Discussion volume | Data + AI intelligence + quality discussion |
| User goal | General conversation | Better investing decisions |

**Strategic principle:** Stay complementary to Nairaland, not competitive with it.

---

## 2. Target User

**Primary:** Intermediate → advanced Nigerian investors  
**Secondary:** Ambitious beginners who want to move beyond casual advice

**Characteristics:**
- Already invest or actively learning to invest in Nigerian equities, T-bills, bonds, mutual funds
- Care about performance, allocation, and informed decision-making
- Prefer signal over noise
- Value a clean, modern product experience

---

## 3. Core Product Pillars

### 1. Portfolio Tracking (Unique Value)
Users can track Nigerian stocks and fixed-income instruments, see cost basis vs current value, unrealized P&L, and asset allocation.

**Current support:**
- Equities (stocks)
- Federal T-Bills

**Future expansion candidates:**
- Bonds
- Mutual funds / money market funds
- Real estate investment vehicles (selective)

### 2. AI Market Intelligence
Gemini-powered market news and analysis with:
- Sentiment classification (Positive / Neutral / Negative)
- Investor impact summary (“Alpha Impact”)
- Clear, actionable framing

This is a key differentiator versus pure discussion forums.

### 3. Focused Investor Community
A discussion space that stays **strictly within investing**.

**Allowed categories (current):**
- Stock Analysis
- Investment Strategies
- Fixed Income
- Market Rumours
- Personal Finance
- Beginner Questions
- Portfolio Reviews

**Explicitly out of scope:**
- Politics
- Romance / relationships
- Sports / entertainment
- General lifestyle topics
- Crime / news dump sections

Quality > volume. The community exists to help people invest better, not to recreate a general Nigerian town square.

### 4. Learning Hub
Structured education on the Nigerian financial landscape.

Goal: help users move from “interested” → “competent” investors through curated lessons rather than pure free-form forum advice.

---

## 4. Design Direction

**Feel:** Premium, modern, calm, data-forward  
**Visual language:** Glassmorphism, emerald green + gold accents, generous whitespace, high legibility  
**Reference:** See `PREMIUM_UI_SPEC.md`

Tone of voice in product copy should feel:
- Confident but not hype-driven
- Intelligent
- Professional
- Respectful of the user’s money and time

---

## 5. Feature Scope Rules

### In scope
- Portfolio tools and performance visibility
- AI-assisted market intelligence
- Investment-only community features
- Financial education content
- Investor-relevant profiles / light reputation (future)
- Analytics that help users understand their positions and the market

### Out of scope (for now)
- General-purpose forum features that pull the product toward Nairaland
- Politics, entertainment, romance, or lifestyle sections
- Classifieds / marketplace
- Heavy social graph features (followers, public feeds, etc.) unless they clearly serve investing outcomes

---

## 6. Success Criteria (Directionally)

NairaIntel is succeeding when:

1. Users regularly update and check their portfolio
2. Market Intelligence feels more useful than scrolling random news
3. Community discussions stay high-signal and investment-focused
4. New users can learn faster because of the Learning Hub
5. The product feels distinctly more premium and focused than a general forum

---

## 7. Monetization Direction (Later)

Possible paths (not yet implemented):
- Premium subscription (advanced analytics, deeper AI insights, exclusive research)
- Targeted financial product advertising (highly relevant, non-spammy)
- Sponsored research / institutional insights
- Data / intelligence products

Monetization must never compromise the premium, high-signal feel.

---

## 8. Guiding Principles

1. **Stay narrow and deep** — Investing only.
2. **Signal over noise** — Prefer quality of insight to quantity of posts.
3. **Data + AI as differentiators** — Discussion alone is not enough.
4. **Premium experience** — Visual and interaction quality should match the seriousness of managing money.
5. **Complement, don’t compete with Nairaland** — Different job, different product.
